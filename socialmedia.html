HENDRIX

Role — Graphic Designer
Format — Social / Digital
Focus — Promotion & Storytelling

Created a range of graphics for Hendrix College Baseball, including game-day announcements, conference/postseason content, player recognition, and program milestones. These posts were made through Adobe Photoshop for Hendrix Baseball Instagram from February to May 2026.
